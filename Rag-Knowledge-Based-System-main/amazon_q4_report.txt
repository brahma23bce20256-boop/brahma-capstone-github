# Amazon Quarterly Financial Report - Q4 2024

## Executive Summary
Amazon reported Q4 2024 revenue of $187.8 billion, a 14% increase year-over-year.
AWS cloud revenue hit $28.8 billion with 19% growth. Advertising revenue reached $17.3 billion.
North America segment showed strong performance with $115.6 billion in sales.

## Key Metrics
- Total Revenue: $187.8B (▲14% YoY)
- Operating Income: $21.2B (▲61% YoY)
- Free Cash Flow: $38.2B
- AWS Revenue: $28.8B (▲19%)
- Advertising Revenue: $17.3B (▲18%)

## Risk Factors
Regulatory compliance remains a priority with new FTC guidelines effective January 2025.
Supply chain disruptions in APAC region impacted Q4 delivery metrics by 2.3%.
Litigation pending: EU antitrust investigation into marketplace practices.
Amazon faces increased competition from Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud in enterprise segment.
Currency exchange volatility in Latin America and EMEA regions affected international revenue by $1.2B.

## Outlook
Q1 2025 guidance: Revenue $158-163B. AWS growth expected to accelerate with AI/ML services.
New partnership with NVIDIA for AI training infrastructure. Expansion into healthcare analytics.
