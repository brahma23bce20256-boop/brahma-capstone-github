# HR Employee Satisfaction Survey - Q4 2024

## Department: Engineering
Response Rate: 85% (340/400 employees)
- Overall satisfaction: 4.2/5 (positive)
- Work-life balance: 3.8/5 (neutral)
- Compensation: 4.5/5 (positive)
- Career growth: 4.0/5 (positive)
Comments: "Great AI projects with NVIDIA partnership. Cloud migration to Azure improved productivity. Need better supply chain integration tools."

## Department: Sales
Response Rate: 78% (195/250 employees)
- Overall satisfaction: 3.5/5 (neutral)
- Work-life balance: 2.8/5 (negative)
- Compensation: 4.2/5 (positive)
- Career growth: 3.2/5 (neutral)
Comments: "Revenue targets increased 15% but headcount frozen. Compliance training taking too much time. Regional teams in APAC and EMEA need more support."

## Department: Support
Response Rate: 72% (180/250 employees)
- Overall satisfaction: 2.9/5 (negative)
- Work-life balance: 2.5/5 (negative)
- Compensation: 3.1/5 (neutral)
- Career growth: 2.8/5 (negative)
Comments: "Ticket volume up 40% after Black Friday outage. Shopify incident caused major burnout. Need better SLA management tools. Microsoft Dynamics integration failed."
